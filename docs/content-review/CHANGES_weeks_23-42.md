Main sources:
- Term definitions: ACOG Committee Opinion 579, https://www.acog.org/clinical/clinical-guidance/committee-opinion/articles/2013/11/definition-of-term-pregnancy (early term 37+0, full term 39+0, late term 41+0, post term 42+0)
