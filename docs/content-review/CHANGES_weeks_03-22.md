## (c) Kept, but not 100% sure

- **Weights for weeks 10–13 (4 g, 45 g, 58 g, 73 g), not changed as instructed.** Mayo gives about 8 g at week 11 and about 14 g at week 12, and Hadlock starts at week 14. Week 11's 45 g and week 12's 58 g look several times too high. Worth a review. Week 11's screw and week 12's Zippo fit the real size better than the listed weight. I kept both, so if the weights are corrected, the objects still fit.
- Week 20 "about the length of a regular screwdriver, head to heel". Crown-heel is about 25 cm, and screwdrivers vary.
- Week 17 brown fat and vernix "starting". Sources put them anywhere from week 17 to 20.
- Week 15 lanugo and reaction to light. Sources vary from week 14 to 18.
- Weeks 16–18 hearing. Cleveland says week 16, other sources say 18–20. The text is hedged ("in the coming weeks" / "around these weeks").
- Week 18 "blood pressure tends to drop a bit in the second trimester". Well established, but stated as a mechanism.
- Week 21: the 24–28 week glucose test window (standard ACOG/Israeli practice, not checked on an Israeli site). "Doubles weight within about a month" (Hadlock 399 g at week 21 to about 780 g at week 25, so OK).
- Week 9 NIPT "usually done privately". General knowledge, no amounts given.
