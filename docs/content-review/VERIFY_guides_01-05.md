## 3. Still uncertain
- **Kupa differences.** I checked the copay and funding details mostly on Maccabi and Kol Zchut (Kol Zchut is national). Clalit, Meuhedet and Leumit amounts and procedures may differ. The texts are general ("בדרך כלל", "כדאי לבדוק בקופה").
- **First biochemical screen "כלולה בסל".** Maccabi's basic basket page says no cost. Kol Zchut doesn't list it separately, so I couldn't confirm that it is free at every kupa.
- **Beta referral.** Whether a referral is needed varies by kupa. It is already hedged.
- **Fetal echo.** The new timing (from ~19, sometimes earlier) is backed by Clalit and Maccabi. Coverage varies: it is usually funded with a medical indication and otherwise private. The text says "check what's covered".
- **"More frequent visits toward the end" (test_4_2).** I verified the general pattern on NHS, not on an Israeli MoH schedule.
- **NIPT basket status.** It is not in the basket as of the sources found. If a future basket decision adds it, test_3_4 will need an update.
- The kolzchut.org.il and gov.il pages block WebFetch. I read them through curl (HTTP 200).
