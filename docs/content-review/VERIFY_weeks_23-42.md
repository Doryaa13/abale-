## Medical-safety pass

The remaining allowed warnings are all generic "מתקשרים לרופא / נוסעים למיון / פונים להיבדק":
- reduced movements (29, 38, 40, 42)
- contractions (26, 32, 36)
- swelling (26, 34)
- breathlessness (29)

The text has no doses, thresholds, counting rules, supplements, remedies or diet rules. I removed the toilet-stool constipation remedy (27). "שלה בלי אלכוהול" (24) and "תשאלו את הצוות מה מותר לה לשתות" (39) are left as obvious, non-clinical points. The humor is checked, and none of it is at her expense.

- The GBS window differs: 35–37 (MoH circular) vs 35–38 (Maccabi). The text says "around 35–37", which is fine.
- Israeli follow-up after the due date (frequency, kupa vs hospital) varies and has no official page. The text stays generic ("מעקב צמוד", "תבררו איפה ומתי"), which is correct.
- Week 31 "all senses active to some degree" and "detects light", and week 34 "vernix covers the body" and "about 200 g a week" (Hadlock 34→35 is +218 g), are consumer-site level claims but hedged. I kept them.
- babySize brand names (Makita in 25, Blundstone in 31, PS5 in 38) are outside my edit scope. I left them for the editor.
- Week 41 "dry or peeling skin" is documented mainly for 42+ (postterm). It is hedged with "לפעמים" and kept.
