## (d) Factual claims in articles and their sources

- Early anatomy scan is not in the basic basket and the sex can often be seen there (4_1, 1_4, 2_4 say "some tests are not in the basic basket"). Source: https://www.maccabi4u.co.il/maccabi_circles/pregnancy-and-birth/pregnancy_birth/tests/first-anatomy-scan/ ; NIPT is also private: https://www.maccabi4u.co.il/maccabi_circles/pregnancy-and-birth/pregnancy_birth/tests/nipt/
- Some supplementary plans give a discount or refund on pregnancy tests (1_4). Source: the same Maccabi pages (refund via "חבילת הריון ולידה" in some tiers).
- Some first-trimester tests have a short time window (3_1). Source: https://www.maccabi4u.co.il/eligibilites/1650/
- "Couvade syndrome" / "sympathetic pregnancy" is a recognized term (3_2). This is a well-established term. The claim about a testosterone study was removed.
- Rights (1_4, 2_4, 3_4) are deliberately not stated. The articles only point readers to Kol Zchut and Bituah Leumi.
- Hedged generalizations, not stated as facts: "אצל הרבה נשים" nausea, tiredness and smell sensitivity (2_1); "אצל הרבה נשים השליש השני מביא קצת אוויר" (4_3); many couples announce around the end of month 3 (3_3); childbirth-prep courses for religious couples exist (5_2, general knowledge).
- The only medical advice used is generic: "מתקשרים לרופא" (1_3, 2_1, 5_1), "מתקשרים לרופא או נוסעים למיון" (5_1), "talk to the family doctor or a professional" about the dad's stress (2_2) and his own symptoms (3_2), and "check with the doctor before long trips or flights" (4_3).

## (e) Things I'm unsure about

- Eligibility rules for amnio and CVS funding: the Maccabi page says the threshold is 32+ at conception since 14.3.2024. Other kupot may differ, so the exam text only says "depends on age and on a genetic counselor's recommendation".
- Fetal echo timing: I couldn't find an official Israeli window. I loosened it to "around week 20 on".
- Whether a beta hCG referral is required varies by kupa, so it is hedged.
- First-trimester tests and fasting: not verified, so the text says "check the referral".
- I checked mostly Maccabi pages. Clalit, Meuhedet and Leumit may have different copays and coverage. The texts use "בחלק מהקופות" and "כדאי לבדוק".
- The late anatomy scan (weeks ~20–24, in the basket) is not in month 5. It sits in month 6 in guides_06-09, so I didn't add it.
- Photo choices are my judgment. I don't know what each image actually shows (e.g. "month2", "trimesters").
- 4_3 suggests "check with the doctor before long trips or flights". This is generic, but remove it if even that is too medical.
